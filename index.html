01 — About

What is Agent-Thon?

CGC Agent-Thon 2026 is a one-day internal hackathon where mixed teams of CGC professionals and university students build AI-powered agent solutions using Microsoft Copilot.

Hosted at Microsoft's Kuala Lumpur office, this event is more than a competition — it is a launchpad. The most promising prototypes will move into pilot implementation, directly impacting CGC's business operations.

Before the main event, participants attend two intensive bootcamps: a Copilot Technical Training led by Microsoft experts, and a Business Use-Case Workshop anchored by CGC's own subject matter experts. Ongoing mentoring runs through to event day.

This is CGC's commitment to building an AI-first culture — from the ground up, by the people who know the business best.
